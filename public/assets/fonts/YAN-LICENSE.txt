Jinling Yan Light is a renamed, lighter WOFF2 subset of I.Ngaan 1.004 under GPL-2.0-or-later.
Modification: fixed-book character subset, webfont format, renamed family, and modest outline thinning with FreeType strength -8 on a 1024-unit em; original advance widths retained.
This lighter weight was made for this book, not published as a Light weight by Ichiten Fonts Project.
The source directory contains the original TTF, character list and rebuild script.
